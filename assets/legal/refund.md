# Refund Policy

**Effective date:** 2 August 2026

**Document version:** 1

Atlas is developed and operated by Pranta Dutta ("we", "us"). We want Atlas to be genuinely worth paying for. If it is not right for you, here is how refunds work.

---

## The short version

- Purchases are handled by the platform you obtained Atlas from, so **refunds are requested through that platform**, not through us.
- Every paid feature has a **free tier you can try first**. There is no trial to forget about, and no charge until you decide.
- If a refund request is outside the platform's window and you feel the outcome is unfair, **write to us anyway** and we will do what we reasonably can.

---

## 1. How purchases work

Atlas offers paid access as a recurring subscription (billed monthly or yearly) and as a one-time lifetime purchase. All payments are collected and processed by the distribution platform through which you obtained Atlas. We never receive or store your payment card details.

Because that platform is the merchant of record, **it controls the refund process and the applicable time limits**.

## 2. How to request a refund

Use the purchase or order history section of the platform you bought through and follow its refund request process. Requests are assessed under that platform's own policy, and its decision is generally final.

You will usually need your order or transaction reference, which appears on the receipt sent to you at the time of purchase.

## 3. Statutory rights

If you are a consumer, you may have a legal right to cancel or obtain a refund under the law of your country, and that right applies regardless of anything in this policy. Some jurisdictions provide a cooling-off period for digital purchases; where that applies to you, you may exercise it. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot be limited by agreement.

## 4. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel a recurring subscription at any time in the subscription settings provided by the platform you purchased through. The app also provides a shortcut to that screen.

Cancelling stops future renewals. It does **not** by itself refund the current period. Your paid access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for, and then stops. We do not provide pro-rated refunds for the unused part of a billing period unless the law requires it.

## 5. Lifetime purchases

A lifetime purchase is a one-time payment for continued access to the paid features for as long as we operate Atlas. It is not a promise that the service will run forever. If we discontinue Atlas entirely, we will give reasonable notice; if that happens unreasonably soon after your purchase, contact us and we will consider a fair, pro-rated remedy.

## 6. When we may decline

We may decline a discretionary refund where a request is repeated or appears abusive, where the account has breached the Terms of Service, or where access has already been used substantially over an extended period. This does not affect your statutory rights.

## 7. Problems that are our fault

If a paid feature is broken, unavailable for an extended period, or does not work as described, that is on us. Tell us what happened. We would rather fix the problem or arrange a fair remedy than have you out of pocket.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will update the version number above and ask you to review and accept the updated documents in the app before you continue using it.
